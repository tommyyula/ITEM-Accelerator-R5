验收基线Acceptance baseline

  • 分母：确定样本集合与业务边界。
  • 周期：约定运营周期。
  • 错误级别：语义、规则/计算、权限/流程、数据质量、结果记录。
  • Denominator: fix the sample set and the business boundary.
  • Period: agree the operating period.
  • Error levels: semantics, rules/calculation, permissions/process, data quality, result records.
